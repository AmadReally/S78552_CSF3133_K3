About UMT

History of UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT), formerly known as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM), is a university in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It was officially chartered on 1 February 2007.

The university began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science at Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu. It provided facilities for students and lecturers of the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes to conduct practical sessions and research.

The Cabinet of Malaysia approved the establishment of Terengganu University College on 5 May 1999. It was renamed KUSTEM on 20 June 2001, and on 1 February 2007 it became a full-fledged university. In 2015, the university was granted autonomy status.
